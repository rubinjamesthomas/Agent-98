AGENT 98

Build the perfect addition. Draw, discard, arrange your cards and use the vertical addition algorithm to hit the target.

Mission Settings

10–99 = 2-digit + 2-digit. 100–999 = 3-digit + 3-digit.
Default is hidden. In CPU mode this reveals Mr Thomas's hand too.

How to Play

  1. Each player starts with 4 cards in 2-digit mode or 6 cards in 3-digit mode.
  2. The deck has 52 cards: five of every digit 0–9 plus two purple Jokers.
  3. On your turn, draw from the hidden deck or take the face-up discard, then discard one card.
  4. Enter the two numbers you want to build using the two number fields. The grid mirrors your entry.
  5. A Joker may represent any digit 0–9. The app checks that your two numbers can actually be made from your cards.
  6. Calculate the total yourself using the vertical algorithm and enter it in the answer box.
  7. You get one valid equation submission per turn. A failed equation passes play to the other player.
  8. If the equation equals the target, the game ends immediately. If the deck runs out, the closest submitted equation wins.

TARGET 98
AGENT 98

A two-player number-building mission. Draw, discard, arrange your digits and use the vertical addition algorithm to hit the target exactly.

Mission Settings

10–99 uses 2-digit + 2-digit. 100–999 uses 3-digit + 3-digit.

How to Play

  1. Each player starts with the required number of cards: 4 for 2-digit mode or 6 for 3-digit mode.
  2. The deck has 52 cards: five of each digit 0–9 plus two Jokers.
  3. On your turn, take the top discard or a hidden card from the deck, then discard one card.
  4. Arrange your hand into two numbers in the grid. A Joker may stand for any digit 0–9.
  5. You get one equation submission per turn. You must commit to the Joker's value when you submit.
  6. The app never tells you what a Joker equals. Work it out using the vertical algorithm.
  7. If your equation equals the target, the mission ends immediately.
  8. If the draw pile runs out, the player whose current submitted equation is closest to the target wins.
